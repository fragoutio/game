Turn. Tap for small moves, hold to turn faster.

W W
Double-tap W and keep holding it to run. Let go of W to stop. You can't shoot while running.
Shift or C
While running: slide. Otherwise: crouch (hold). Crouching makes you a smaller target.
1 / 2
Alternator / shotgun. The shotgun hits hard up close (4 health to the body, 5 to the head — a one-shot knockout) but drops to about 1 health from further away, won't fire at long range, and pumps between shots.
Q / E while crouched
Aim down the sight (1.3× zoom): steadier recoil, finer turning. Only works while crouched; stand up and you zoom back out.
Q / E while aiming
Lean left / right to shoot around corners. Tap the same key again to straighten up.
Space / Caps Lock
Your operator's tactical / ultimate ability (pick an operator in the LOCKER). Tactical has a cooldown; the ultimate charges over time and faster when you deal damage.
Right Shift
Zoom in / out of your sight (while crouched). Left Shift or C crouches.
T
Switch the Alternator's sight: 1.3x HOG ring or 2.5x HCOG (zooms in more, turns slower while aiming). It's remembered.
X
Upstairs, facing a gap in the floor or a window: rappel down (you can shoot on the way). X again lets go.
Ladders
Walk into a ladder to climb it, walk away from it to climb down, or press Tab to let go and drop. Stairs are in the room corners.
Grenades
Hold the key to see where it will go and aim it with the arrow keys. Let go to throw.
R Boomerang
Curves out and flies back to you. Explodes on an enemy. 10 s cooldown, reset instantly if it hits.
G Frag
Bounces off walls and the floor. Explodes after 3 s, or straight away if it touches an enemy. Finish them with your gun. 12 s cooldown.
F Thermite
Makes a wall of fire across your throw where it lands. Burns enemies standing in it. 15 s cooldown.
Headshots
A hit on the head takes 3 of 5 health, a body hit takes 1. Two headshots knock a player out.
Auto-fire
Your gun shoots by itself once the crosshair has stayed on an enemy you can see for a moment (it turns yellow, then red when firing).
Recoil
Every shot kicks your view up and sways it. Hold ↓ while firing to stay on target.
Esc
Pause menu (the match keeps going)
Gamepad
Left stick move, right stick look, Y boomerang, X frag, triggers thermite, D-pad + A in menus
5 body hits, 2 headshots, or 1 headshot + 2 body hits knock a player out. Last team standing wins the round. First to 3 rounds wins.
